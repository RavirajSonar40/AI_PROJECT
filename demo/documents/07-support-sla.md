# Support Service Level Agreement (SLA) — Nova Retail

## Support channels
- Email: soporte@novaretail.example
- Phone: available 24/7 only for Enterprise plan customers.
- In-app chat: 08:00 to 22:00, every day.

## Response targets
| Severity | Description | First response | Resolution target |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | Service down or data loss | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | Critical functionality degraded | 1 hour | 8 hours |
| P3 | Functional bug with a workaround | 4 hours | 3 working days |
| P4 | Question or enhancement | 1 working day | 10 working days |

## Coverage hours
P1 and P2 have 24x7 coverage for Enterprise and Premium customers.
Basic customers have coverage Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 Madrid time.

## Escalation
If a P1 is not picked up within 15 minutes it escalates automatically to the on-call engineer.
After 60 minutes without resolution the Head of Operations is notified.

## Service credits
If the monthly SLA is missed, the customer receives credits:
- 99.9% availability: 10% of the monthly fee.
- Below 99.5%: 25% of the monthly fee.
- Below 99.0%: 50% of the monthly fee.

Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the month.

## Maintenance
Scheduled maintenance windows are Sundays 02:00 to 05:00 CET
and are announced 72 hours in advance. They do not count as unavailability.

## Escalation contacts
The escalation contact list is updated quarterly by the Customer Success Manager.
