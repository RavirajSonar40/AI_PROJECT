# Onboarding Guide — Nova Retail

## Before day one
PeopleHub sends the digital contract and bank details form 7 days before the start date.
IT prepares the laptop and ships it to the given address within 48 hours.

## Day 1 — Welcome
09:00 Welcome meeting with the direct manager.
09:30 Credentials issued: corporate email, Slack, PeopleHub and VPN.
10:30 Culture and values session (45 minutes).
12:00 Lunch with the team.
15:00 Workstation setup with the assigned buddy.

## Week 1
- Complete the mandatory information security course (90 minutes).
- Read the vacation, remote work and refund policies.
- Hold a 30-minute meeting with each person on the immediate team.
- Take on a first small task assigned by the manager.

## First month
The goal for the first month is to understand the product and ship one small, low-risk improvement.
A follow-up meeting with People and the manager takes place at 30 days.

## Probation period
Probation is 6 months for technical roles and 2 months for all other roles.
During probation the notice period is 15 days.

## Buddy
Every new joiner gets a buddy for the first 60 days.
The buddy answers day-to-day questions and introduces key people from other areas.

## Access by role
- Engineering: Git repository, staging environment, metrics dashboard.
- Sales: CRM, price catalogue, order dashboard.
- Support: help desk, knowledge base, customer history.

Access is requested in AccessHub and approved within a maximum of 2 working days.

## Offboarding checklist
When someone leaves, IT revokes all access on the same day and collects equipment within 5 working days.
