# Remote Work Policy — Nova Retail

## Hybrid model
Nova Retail runs a hybrid model of 3 remote days and 2 office days per week.
Store and logistics teams are excluded from the hybrid model because their work is on-site.

Office days are agreed per team and must include either Tuesday or Thursday
to make joint meetings possible. The schedule is published every quarter in the team space.

## Flexible hours
Core hours, when everyone must be available, are 10:00 to 16:00 local time.
Outside core hours each person organises their own day, completing 8 hours.

No one is expected to answer messages outside working hours.
Urgent communications must be tagged URGENT in Slack and justified.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop, a 27-inch monitor and 150 euros per year for office supplies.
Furniture (ergonomic chair and desk) is requested once and becomes the employee's property after 2 years.

## Allowance
A remote-work allowance of 40 euros per month is paid, tax-exempt,
to cover internet, electricity and connectivity.

## Security while remote
- The corporate VPN is mandatory to access internal systems.
- Working from public networks without the VPN is forbidden.
- Screens must lock automatically after 5 minutes.
- Personal USB drives are not allowed.

## Exceptions
Requests to work from abroad require approval from the People Directorate
and are limited to 30 calendar days per year for tax and security reasons.

## Review
This policy is reviewed every 12 months. The last update was 3 February 2026.
