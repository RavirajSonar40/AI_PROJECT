# Vacation Policy — Nova Retail

## Available days
All employees on a permanent contract receive 25 working days of vacation per year.
Fixed-term staff accrue 2.5 days per month worked, pro-rated.

Vacation days accrue from 1 January and can be taken until 31 March of the following year.
Days not taken before that date are lost unless the People Directorate grants an explicit exception.

## Requesting and approving
Vacation is requested through the internal PeopleHub tool at least 15 calendar days in advance.
The direct manager must approve or reject the request within a maximum of 5 working days.

During peak season (15 June to 15 September for stores, 1 to 20 December for logistics)
no more than 10 consecutive days may be requested per person and team.

## Special leave
- Marriage or registered partnership: 15 calendar days.
- Birth of a child: 16 weeks for both parents.
- Death of a first-degree relative: 5 working days.
- Moving house: 1 working day.
- Official exams: the day of the exam.

## Seniority
From the fifth year of service, employees receive 1 additional day per year worked,
up to a maximum of 5 extra days. These extra days cannot be carried over between years.

## Sick leave during vacation
If an employee goes on sick leave during vacation, the affected days are returned to their balance,
provided the sick leave lasts more than 3 days and is reported in PeopleHub within the first 48 hours.
