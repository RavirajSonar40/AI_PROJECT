# Refund and Returns Policy — Nova Retail

## General window
Customers may return any product within 30 calendar days of delivery,
without having to give a reason.

## Product condition
The product must be unused, with tags, in its original packaging.
Personal hygiene items and underwear cannot be returned for health reasons.

## How to request a return
1. Open the customer area and select the order.
2. Choose the items and the return reason.
3. Download the prepaid shipping label.
4. Drop the parcel at any collection point within 7 days.

## Refunds
The refund is issued to the original payment method within 5 to 10 working days
from receipt of the parcel at the warehouse.

If the payment was made with a gift card, the amount is returned as gift card balance.

## Free returns
Returns are free for Premium and Enterprise customers.
Basic customers pay a 4.95 euro return shipping cost, deducted from the refund.

## Exchanges
Size or colour exchanges are free and are processed as a return plus a new order,
keeping the original price if the exchange is requested within the first 15 days.

## Defective products
If a product arrives defective, the customer may choose a full refund or a replacement.
Nova Retail always covers shipping and applies no deduction.

## Legal warranty
All products carry a 3-year legal warranty in Spain from delivery.

## Exceptions
Returns are not accepted for personalised products, perishable food
or items bought in a final sale marked "no returns".
