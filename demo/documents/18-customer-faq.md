# Customer FAQ

## How long does delivery take?
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 working days.
Next-day delivery is available in mainland Spain for 6.95 euros.

## Can I change my order after paying?
Yes, as long as the order has not left the warehouse.
The address or items can be changed from the customer area.
Once shipped, you must wait for delivery and request a return.

## How do I track my order?
Every order generates a tracking number that is emailed to you.
It can also be followed in real time from the customer area.

## Do products have a warranty?
Yes. The legal warranty is 3 years in Spain from the delivery date.
Defective products are replaced or refunded at no cost.

## Can I pay with a gift card?
Yes. Gift cards are valid for 24 months from issue
and can be combined with another payment method.

## Do you ship internationally?
We currently ship to Spain, Portugal, France and Italy.
International delivery takes 5 to 8 working days and does not include customs.

## How do I contact support?
By email at soporte@novaretail.example, by in-app chat
or by phone if your plan is Enterprise.

## What if the product arrives damaged?
Attach a photo within the first 48 hours.
Nova Retail covers return shipping and sends a priority replacement.

## Can I cancel my subscription?
Yes, from the customer panel at any time and with no penalty on monthly plans.
Annual plans do not refund the pro-rated part already used.

## Where can I find my invoices?
In the customer panel, Billing section.
Invoices are available as PDF from the first day of the month.

## Is my data stored?
Data is processed under GDPR and is not shared with third parties without consent.
Customers can request deletion of their account and personal data.

## Do you offer volume discounts?
Yes. From 25 users on Enterprise plans a 15% discount applies.
For larger volumes, contact the sales team.
