# Billing, Payments and Subscriptions — Nova Retail

## Billing cycle
Subscriptions are billed monthly in advance on the 1st of each month.
The invoice is issued as a PDF and emailed the same day.

## Accepted payment methods
- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express).
- SEPA bank transfer for amounts above 500 euros.
- Direct debit for Enterprise customers with an annual contract.

Cash and cryptocurrencies are not accepted.

## Available plans
| Plan | Monthly price | Included users | Storage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 29 euros | 3 | 50 GB |
| Premium | 89 euros | 10 | 500 GB |
| Enterprise | 299 euros | 50 | 2 TB |

Additional users cost 9 euros per month on Basic, 7 on Premium and 5 on Enterprise.

## Taxes
Prices are shown excluding VAT. The 21% Spanish VAT rate applies to customers in Spain.
EU customers with a valid VAT number are exempt.

## Plan changes
Upgrades can be made at any time and are charged pro-rated.
Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, it is retried after 3, 7 and 14 days.
After 14 days without success the account becomes read-only.
After 30 days the account is suspended and data is kept for 90 days.

## Cancellation
Cancellation can be done from the customer panel at any time.
There is no penalty for cancelling a monthly plan; annual plans do not refund the unused pro-rated part.

## Billing details
Tax details are updated in the panel and apply to the next invoice issued.
Corrective invoices are issued within a maximum of 5 working days.
