# Employee Benefits — Nova Retail

## Health insurance
Nova Retail covers 100% of the private health insurance premium for the employee
and 60% for spouse and children. Cover starts on the first day of employment.

The provider network includes 2,400 centres across Spain and basic dental cover.
The digital health card is downloaded from the insurer's app.

## Training
Every employee has an annual training budget of 1,200 euros.
It can be spent on courses, certifications, books or conferences.

Technical certifications costing more than 500 euros require a 12-month commitment to stay with the company.

## Meal card
A meal card is loaded with 11 euros per working day worked.
The balance is topped up on the first day of each month and expires after 12 months.

## Pension plan
The company contributes 3% of annual gross salary to the employee pension plan.
Employees may contribute voluntarily up to the legal tax-relief limit.

## Wellbeing
- Gym: 50% of the monthly fee is subsidised, up to 30 euros.
- Physiotherapy sessions at the head office every Wednesday.
- Psychological support programme: 6 free sessions per year, fully confidential.

## Discounts
Employees get 20% off in all Nova stores and in the online store,
combinable with sales but not with other promotions. The limit is 3,000 euros of purchases per year.

## Flexible pay
Up to 30% of gross salary can be allocated to flexible pay:
transport, childcare, additional health insurance and training.

## Seniority
At 3 years a 1,000 euro bonus is paid, and at 5 years a team trip.
Bonuses are subject to the applicable tax withholding.
